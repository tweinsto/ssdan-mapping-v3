This site makes U.S. demographic and economic trends easy to explore through interactive maps that move from national patterns down to state and county detail. Clicking on a state reveals related charts and tables that connect local statistics to broader trends. Brought to you by the Social Science Data Analysis Network (SSDAN) at the University of Michigan, it is designed to make Census data clear and useful for both professionals and general audiences.
